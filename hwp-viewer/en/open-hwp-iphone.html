How to open HWP files on iPhone

Checked 2026-11-16 · Written by ONGSIM, the maker of HWP Viewer

Support · 지원Privacy Policy · 개인정보 처리방침Guides

To open an HWP file on iPhone, install an app that supports it from the App Store. Free apps such as Hancom Office Viewer or HWP Viewer are enough for reading. For a file in Mail or KakaoTalk, tap Share and choose that app.

Steps

  1. Install an HWP app from the App Store (search “hwp”).
  2. Mail: touch and hold the attachment, tap Share, then choose the app.
  3. KakaoTalk: open the file, tap the share (export) button, then choose the app.
  4. Files app: open the app and pick the document from inside it.

FAQ

Can I open HWP files on iPhone for free?

Yes. Hancom Office Viewer, Hancom Docs and HWP Viewer are free on the App Store.

How do I open an HWP file I got on KakaoTalk?

Open the file in KakaoTalk, tap the share (export) button and choose an app that opens HWP.

Can I edit HWP files on iPhone?

Some apps can. Hancom Docs and Mekul list editing in the App Store, and HWP Viewer fills in forms and fixes text for free.

Can I turn an HWP file into a PDF on iPhone?

Yes. Hancom Office Viewer converts to PDF or PNG, and saving a PDF is free in HWP Viewer.
