How to open HWP files on a Mac

Checked 2026-11-16 · Written by ONGSIM, the maker of HWP Viewer

Support · 지원Privacy Policy · 개인정보 처리방침Guides

HWP and HWPX files come from Hangul, the Korean word processor made by Hancom. Apple's guide to converting files in Pages does not mention HWP, so install an app that reads them: Hancom's free Hangul Viewer from the Mac App Store to read, or an editor such as Hangul for macOS, Mekul, HWP Viewer & Editor - Hangul or the open-source HOP to make changes.

Steps

  1. Decide whether you only need to read the file or also edit and send it back.
  2. Install an app from the table below. Searching “hwp” in the Mac App Store finds most of them.
  3. In Finder, Control-click the file, choose Open With, then pick the app. To always use it, press Cmd-I, choose the app under Open with, then click Change All.

Converting to PDF without an app

Online converters such as Smallpdf and CloudConvert turn HWP into PDF. Smallpdf says uploaded files are deleted after one hour; CloudConvert's free tier is 10 credits a day. Your file goes to their servers, so avoid this for documents with personal details.

Things to know

  • Apple's guide to converting files in Pages does not mention HWP.
  • HWP Viewer's menus are in Korean.
  • Distribution-only HWP files can be viewed but not saved as PDF or printed in HWP Viewer.
  • Fonts differ between apps, so line breaks can shift.

FAQ

What is an HWP file?

HWP is the document format of Hangul, the Korean word processor by Hancom. HWPX is the newer XML-based format that follows the Korean standard KS X 6101.

Can I open HWP files on a Mac for free?

Yes. Hancom Office HWP Viewer, Mekul and HWP Viewer are free in the Mac App Store, and HOP is free open-source software.

Can Pages open HWP files?

Apple's guide to converting files in Pages does not mention HWP. Use an app that supports HWP.

Can Google Drive preview HWP files?

Google Drive's help page on previewable file types does not list HWP or HWPX. You can still store the files there.
